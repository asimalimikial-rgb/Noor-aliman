# Privacy Policy — Noor Al-Iman

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Noor Al-Iman does not require an account and does not operate an app-owned user
profile or advertising tracker.

## Data and permissions

- **Location:** If the user chooses current-location features, Android location
  permission is used to calculate prayer times and Qibla direction. The app stores
  the selected coordinates locally on the device so the selected location can be
  reused. Location is not sent to an app-owned server by the Android alarm code.
- **Camera:** The camera is used only when the user opens the optional camera-based
  Qibla view and grants camera permission.
- **Notifications and exact alarms:** These permissions are used to schedule and
  display prayer alerts, reminders, and timed dhikr.
- **Local preferences and downloads:** Settings, prayer adjustments, and content
  downloaded for offline use are stored on the device.
- **Online content:** Quran text, recitations, and other explicitly requested
  online resources may be fetched from their named providers. Those providers may
  receive normal network metadata such as IP address under their own policies.

## Sharing and retention

The app developer does not receive the locally stored settings or location through
an app-operated account service. Data stored by the app remains on the device until
the user clears app storage or uninstalls the app. Android permissions can be
revoked at any time in system settings.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the app developer through the contact details
published with the Noor Al-Iman project.
